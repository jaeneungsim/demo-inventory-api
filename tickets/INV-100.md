## Requirements
- R1: `GET /items` lists the items with their id, name and price.
- R2: `GET /items/{id}` returns one item, or 404 when it does not exist.
- R3: `POST /items` creates an item and rejects invalid input with 400.
- R4: The web app lists the items and shows an item detail page.
